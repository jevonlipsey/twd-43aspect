The Walking Dead Definitive 4:3 Patcher

Makes The Walking Dead: The Telltale Definitive Series fill 4:3 screens (Retroid Pocket, DroidDeck, 4:3 monitors) with no black bars: the 3D scenes keep their full width and show more above and below, and in all five games the labels, notifications, choice prompts and the reticle stay inside the 4:3 view.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your WDC.exe and game archive are never uploaded and never leave this device.

Install it

  1. Close the game. In the game folder, rename your original WDC.exe to WDC.exe.bak. Keep it as your backup.
  2. Unzip TWD-4x3-patch.zip into the game folder. It adds the new WDC.exe and ten new files inside Archives; nothing else is replaced. On DroidDeck the game folder is /mnt/droiddeck-sd/steamapps/common/The Walking Dead The Telltale Definitive Series/ (internal storage: /root/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/common/The Walking Dead The Telltale Definitive Series/), and your downloads are in /root/Storage/Download.
  3. Launch the game through Steam as usual: the patched WDC.exe still needs Steam running. Play fullscreen at 1280x960. On DroidDeck, set Resolution to Match screen (1280x960) and 16:9 game fit to Stretch (or off).
  4. Skipping a cutscene: hold RB (R1 on a PlayStation pad; Q on a keyboard) for one second while a cutscene plays. It never skips choices or quick-time events. To drop only the skip, delete the *_PC_P43_SKIP_* files from Archives.
  5. A game update or Steam's “Verify integrity of game files” puts the original WDC.exe back. Patch again afterwards.
  6. To undo: delete the new WDC.exe, rename WDC.exe.bak back to WDC.exe, and delete every _resdesc_50_PC_P43_*.lua (this includes the skip files, if you added them) and WDC_pc_PC_P43_*.ttarch2 file from Archives.
