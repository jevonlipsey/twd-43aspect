The Walking Dead Definitive 4:3 Patcher

Makes The Walking Dead: The Telltale Definitive Series fill 4:3 screens (Retroid Pocket, DroidDeck, 4:3 monitors) with no black bars: the 3D scenes keep their full width, and the interaction label stays inside the view.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your WDC.exe and game archive are never uploaded and never leave this device.

Download TWD-4x3-patch.zip

The zip is laid out like the game folder: WDC.exe, plus two new files in Archives. If you can't unzip on your device, download the three files one by one and put WDC.exe in the game folder and the other two in its Archives folder:

WDC.exe _resdesc_50_PC_P43_S1_01.lua WDC_pc_PC_P43_S1_data.ttarch2

Install it

  1. Close the game. In the game folder, rename your original WDC.exe to WDC.exe.bak. Keep it as your backup.
  2. Unzip TWD-4x3-patch.zip into the game folder. It adds the new WDC.exe and two new files inside Archives; nothing else is replaced. On DroidDeck the game folder is /mnt/droiddeck-sd/steamapps/common/The Walking Dead The Telltale Definitive Series/ (internal storage: /root/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/common/The Walking Dead The Telltale Definitive Series/), and your downloads are in /root/Storage/Download.
  3. Launch the game through Steam as usual: the patched WDC.exe still needs Steam running. Play fullscreen at 1280x960. On DroidDeck, set Resolution to Match screen (1280x960) and 16:9 game fit to Stretch (or off).
  4. A game update or Steam's “Verify integrity of game files” puts the original WDC.exe back. Patch again afterwards.
  5. To undo: delete the new WDC.exe, rename WDC.exe.bak back to WDC.exe, and delete _resdesc_50_PC_P43_S1_01.lua and WDC_pc_PC_P43_S1_data.ttarch2 from Archives.
